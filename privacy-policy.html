1. Introduction

Welcome to Gadget Item. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how Gadget Item collects, uses, stores, protects, and handles your information when you visit our website, create an account, purchase products, or interact with our e-commerce platform.

By using our website, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you have any questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices, please feel free to reach out to our team.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use on our website, Gadget Item may collect different types of data to provide a seamless shopping and delivery experience:

Account Data

  • Email address
  • Authentication and account identifiers
  • Login-related information handled securely through our authentication provider

Order Data

  • Customer name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Delivery address details (Street, House/Apartment, Thana/Upazila, District, Division)
  • Ordered products, quantities, selected variants, and product prices
  • Delivery area and associated delivery charges
  • Applied promotional codes and discounts
  • Order status and transaction timestamps

Cart Information

When you add items to your shopping cart while logged in, your cart data may be associated with your account identifier. This includes Product ID, product name, slug, image, selected variants, quantity, unit price, total price, SKU, category, and date/time added.

Technical Information

Our website technical infrastructure may process limited technical logs when necessary, such as browser type, device information, IP-related technical parameters, and basic website interaction records.

3. How We Use Information

Gadget Item uses collected information for lawful business and operational purposes, ensuring you receive quality service:

  • Order Processing & Fulfillment: Processing items, validating quantities, managing checkouts, and preparing shipments.
  • Delivery Management: Ensuring your packages reach the correct location through reliable delivery partners.
  • Customer Support: Responding to inquiries, addressing queries, and managing returns, refunds, or exchanges.
  • Account Administration: Maintaining your shopping cart, order history, and account profile preferences.
  • Promo Code Verification: Checking eligibility, calculating discounts, and applying relevant promo values to your checkout.
  • Security & Abuse Prevention: Detecting fraudulent transactions, safeguarding store infrastructure, and ensuring operational safety.

4. Order and Delivery Information

When an order requires delivery, necessary customer information must be communicated to logistics partners. We ensure that address information is handled with care:

  • No Public Exposure: Customer address information is never publicly shared, published, or sold to unrelated third parties.
  • Courier Collaboration: We share only the necessary particulars with trusted courier/delivery services strictly for order fulfillment. This includes customer name, phone number, delivery address, Thana/Upazila, District, Division, and delivery instructions.
  • Strictly Limited Scope: Delivery partners receive only the details required to perform the delivery and nothing more. The entire customer database is never exposed or transferred to couriers.

5. How We Share Information

Gadget Item does NOT sell, rent, or trade customer personal information to third parties for commercial resale or advertising. Information is shared only when reasonably necessary:

  • Courier & Delivery Partners: To fulfill physical delivery of your purchased items.
  • Essential Service Providers: Technical infrastructure providers, database operators, and secure authentication platforms necessary to run the platform.
  • Legal Obligations: If required by applicable laws, legal processes, or governmental requests to protect rights, property, or safety.

6. Database Security & Access Control

We take the security of your records seriously. Customer information is stored using secure database infrastructure and controlled access protocols:

  • Customer data is not publicly accessible through our website interface.
  • Unauthorized external users cannot view or modify internal customer records.
  • Administrative entry is strictly restricted. Access is granted only to authorized Owner/Admin personnel and the relevant account holder where applicable.

We Do Not Sell Your Personal Information

Your trust is valuable. Gadget Item maintains strict boundaries regarding customer privacy. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal profile data, shopping history, or delivery addresses.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Gadget Item uses cookies and similar browser storage technologies to maintain user sessions, manage authentication state, support shopping cart functionality, remember preferences, and improve general user experience and website security. You can adjust your browser settings to control cookie preferences, though disabling essential cookies may impact certain website features.

8. Data Retention

We retain customer information for as long as reasonably required to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, including order fulfillment, customer support, maintaining accounting and transaction records, compliance with legal obligations, and resolving disputes. Once data is no longer necessary for operational or legal requirements, it is securely archived or deleted.

9. Your Rights, Data Correction & Deletion

You have the right to review, update, or correct your personal account details through your profile dashboard. If you believe any personal data we hold is inaccurate, or if you wish to inquire about data deletion requests, you may contact our support team. Please note that certain transactional records must be retained for legitimate business, tax, and legal compliance reasons.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website is designed for general audiences and customers seeking electronic gadgets and accessories. We do not knowingly or intentionally target, collect, or solicit personal information from children without appropriate parental or legal guardian authorization.

11. Third-Party Services

To operate smoothly, Gadget Item utilizes reliable third-party infrastructure components, such as secure authentication platforms, database services, image hosting providers, and courier networks. These third parties process information under their respective operational policies and security standards.

12. Security Disclaimer

We maintain reasonable and appropriate technical, administrative, and organizational safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. However, please note that no internet-based system or digital transmission can guarantee absolute security. While we continuously work to minimize risks and protect your information, we cannot promise absolute 100% immunity from all potential security risks.

13. Changes to Privacy Policy

Gadget Item reserves the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be published directly on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.

14. Contact / Privacy Questions

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how your personal information is handled, please feel free to reach out to Gadget Item customer support through our official communication channels or profile assistance page.
