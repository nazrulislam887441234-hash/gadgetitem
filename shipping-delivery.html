Shipping & Delivery

Everything you need to know about delivery areas, delivery charges and order delivery.

Delivery Charges

Delivery charges apply to your orders based on your selected delivery location and the eligibility of each product in your cart.

Inside Dhaka

৳60

Per eligible product

Outside Dhaka

৳120

Per eligible product

If a product has freeDelivery set to true, no delivery charge is added for that product. If a product has freeDelivery set to false, the applicable delivery charge is added for that product.

How Delivery Charges Are Calculated

Delivery charges are calculated per product that is not eligible for free delivery.

Example 1 (Outside Dhaka)

  • Suppose an order contains 5 products.
  • 2 products have freeDelivery = true
  • 3 products have freeDelivery = false
  • Delivery area = Outside Dhaka
  • Calculation: 3 × ৳120 = ৳360 delivery charge.
  • The 2 free-delivery products do not add any delivery charge.

Example 2 (Inside Dhaka)

  • Suppose 4 products are ordered.
  • 2 products have freeDelivery = false.
  • Calculation: 2 × ৳60 = ৳120 delivery charge.
  • The other 2 free-delivery products do not add delivery charges.

Free Delivery Products

Some products may include free delivery. When a product has freeDelivery = true, that product does not contribute to the delivery charge.

Free delivery applies according to the product information shown on the website and may vary by product.

How Delivery Works

Step 1: Place Your Order

Select your products, choose the delivery area and provide your required delivery information.

Step 2: Order Confirmation

Your order information is submitted for processing.

Step 3: Order Processing

Your order is reviewed and prepared for delivery.

Step 4: Delivery

Your order is handed over for delivery to the provided destination.

Step 5: Receive Your Order

Receive the order at the delivery location provided during checkout.

Information Required for Delivery

Customers may be required to provide:

  • Customer name
  • Phone number
  • Full address
  • Thana / Upazila

  • Division
  • Delivery area

Please provide accurate delivery information during checkout so that the order can be delivered correctly.

Please Check Your Address

Before confirming an order, carefully check your name, phone number and delivery address. Incorrect or incomplete information may cause delivery delays or difficulty contacting the customer.

Delivery Delays

Delivery times may sometimes be affected by circumstances outside the website's direct control, including courier operations, weather, traffic, holidays, unexpected events or incorrect delivery information.

Customers should ensure that their contact and address information is correct to help avoid unnecessary delays.
