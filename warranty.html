Warranty Policy

Read Gadget Item's warranty policy, warranty coverage, claim process, exclusions, and support information.

Product-Specific Warranty

"The warranty information displayed on the individual product page takes priority for that specific product."

For example, if a product page says: Warranty: 3 Months, then that product's stated warranty period is 3 months, subject to the applicable warranty conditions.

Warranty Policy Overview

Some products sold through Gadget Item may include a manufacturer or seller warranty, while warranty availability depends entirely on the individual product.

  • Not every product necessarily has a warranty.
  • The warranty period is clearly shown on the individual product page when applicable.
  • Customers should check the product page before placing an order.
  • Warranty coverage is strictly limited to the terms specified for that product.

Warranty Period

The warranty period starts from the date the order is delivered to the customer.

Warranty terms can vary widely across different items. Examples may include:

  • 3 Months
  • 6 Months
  • 12 Months

Please note that the actual warranty period depends entirely on the specific product. Do not assume every product shares the same warranty duration.

What Warranty May Cover

Depending on the product and its stated warranty terms, warranty service may cover manufacturing defects or functional problems that occur under normal intended use.

What Warranty Does Not Cover

Warranty normally does not cover the following conditions:

  • Physical damage caused by the customer
  • Accidental damage
  • Broken parts caused by misuse
  • Water or liquid damage unless explicitly covered
  • Unauthorized modification or repair
  • Damage caused by incorrect installation or use
  • Normal wear and tear
  • Cosmetic damage that does not affect functionality
  • Damage caused by improper handling
  • Damage caused by external electrical problems where applicable
  • Missing accessories reported after delivery
  • Problems caused by using the product outside its intended purpose

Actual warranty eligibility depends entirely on the product's specific warranty terms.

Warranty Claim Process

1

Contact Support

Contact Gadget Item support team through phone or email.

2

Provide Order Information

Provide your order details, product name, and relevant reference information.

3

Explain the Problem

Describe the functional problem clearly and provide requested photos, videos, or evidence.

4

Claim Review & Inspection

Our team reviews the claim. If eligible, the product may be inspected for appropriate warranty support.

Please note: Automatic replacement or repair is not promised before physical verification.

Required Proof

Customers may be asked to provide:

  • Order information
  • Product details
  • Photos
  • Videos showing the problem
  • Other reasonable evidence required to verify the warranty claim

Proof may be required before any warranty claim is formally approved.

Warranty Verification

Gadget Item evaluates and verifies claims by checking:

  • Whether the product has an active warranty
  • Whether the claim falls within the stated warranty period
  • Whether the issue appears to be a manufacturing or functional defect
  • Whether the issue is excluded under the product's specific warranty terms

Warranty Service Outcomes

Depending on the product and situation, eligible warranty claims may result in an appropriate solution such as repair, replacement, or other suitable warranty support.

Specific solutions are not guaranteed prior to physical inspection and verification.

Shipping During Warranty Claims

Shipping and transport arrangements for warranty claims depend on the product, warranty terms, and individual circumstances. Gadget Item does not make an unconditional promise to cover all shipping costs in every scenario.

No Cash Refund Through Warranty

Gadget Item currently does not support online payment processing. Therefore:

  • Warranty claims are not automatically converted into cash refunds.
  • If a warranty issue is approved, resolution will follow the applicable product and warranty terms.
  • Customers should contact support for guidance and assistance.

Warranty vs. Returns

Warranty: Covers eligible product defects and functional issues according to stated warranty terms.

Return: Governed by the separate Return & Refund Policy.
